Bokuchi comes with 9 built-in themes to match your preference. You can also create your own [custom themes](#custom-themes) based on any of them.

## Available Themes

| Theme | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Default** | Light | Clean light theme with blue accents (Material Design) |
| **Dark** | Dark | Dark background with light text, easy on the eyes |
| **Pastel** | Light | Soft, gentle colors with mint green and pink accents |
| **Vivid** | Light | Bold, vibrant colors with orange and purple accents |
| **Dawn** | Light | Warm, soft light theme with a gentle terracotta tint, like a sunrise |
| **Twilight** | Dark | Dark theme with warm, golden tones, like a sunset |
| **Silk** | Light | Refined light monotone with a faint cool tint |
| **Ink** | Dark | Calm dark monotone with a faint cool tint |
| **Darcula** | Dark | Inspired by JetBrains IDEs, dark charcoal with warm syntax colors |

## Changing the Theme

There are two ways to switch themes:

### From Settings

1. Open **Settings** (Cmd+, on macOS / Ctrl+, on Windows/Linux)
2. Go to the **Appearance** section
3. Click a theme card to apply it. Hovering a card shows a small color preview

### From the Status Bar

Click the **theme name** displayed in the status bar at the bottom of the window. This provides a quick way to cycle through themes.

## Custom Themes

Not quite happy with any of the built-in colors? You can create your own theme by duplicating an existing one and adjusting its colors.

1. Open **Settings** > **Appearance**
2. Click **New Theme** in the **Custom Themes** section
3. Choose a base theme — your custom theme starts as a copy of it
4. Adjust the colors in the editor that opens

You can change 7 colors: the window background, panel background, text, secondary text, two accent colors, and the divider color. Each color has a color picker and a hex field (like `#1a2b3c`), and your changes are applied to the whole app immediately so you can see the result as you go.

While editing you can also:

- **Rename** the theme — the name appears in the theme list and the status bar
- **Reset colors to base** — go back to the colors of the theme you started from
- **Delete** the theme when you no longer need it

If a text color and its background are too similar, Bokuchi shows a warning so you notice the text may become hard to read.

<Aside>
Custom themes are saved with your settings and work just like the built-in ones — you can apply them from the theme cards or cycle to them from the status bar.
</Aside>

## Theme Persistence

Your theme choice — including custom themes — is saved automatically and will be restored the next time you open Bokuchi.
